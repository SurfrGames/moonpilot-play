Moon Pilot privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version

Moon Pilot has no accounts, no email sign-up, no advertising trackers and no analytics. It works without sending anything. Two optional features send data, and both are described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your pilot name, best scores, rank, achievements, daily streak and settings. They are kept in your browser's local storage and are removed if you clear site data or uninstall the app.
  • A random identifier created on your device. It contains nothing about you or your device.

What is sent, and when

  • Leaderboard scores. Only if you enter a pilot name. When you finish a flight, the game sends your pilot name, the mission, your score, flight time, fuel left, whether the coach was on, and the random identifier. Your pilot name and score are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name if you do not want it shown.
  • Shared flights. Only if you tick "Share my flights to help train the AI". The game then sends the flight path and controls of each flight (position, speed, direction, throttle and mass over time) with the random identifier. This is used to improve the game's AI pilot. It is off by default and you can untick it at any time.

Scores and shared flights are stored with Supabase, a database provider, on servers in India. Like any website, the hosting and database providers see your IP address when your device connects; the game does not store it.

What is never collected

No email address, real name, location, contacts, photos, payment details or advertising identifiers.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information. Pilot names appear publicly, so children should choose a name that does not identify them.

Removing your data

Clear the game's site data in your browser, or uninstall the app, to remove everything stored on your device. To have a leaderboard entry removed, contact the publisher through the game's store page and give the pilot name.
